Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Moodist is an open-source ambient sound generator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Data Collection

Moodist does not collect, store, or process any personal data. All your settings (such as volume and selected sounds) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

2. Third-Party Services

Some features (like Lofi music) may embed content from third parties such as YouTube. These third parties may collect data according to their own privacy policies. Moodist does not track or control this data.

3. Open Source

This project is open-source. You can review our code on GitHub to verify our privacy practices.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月20日

Moodist 是一款开源的环境声音生成器。我们尊重您的隐私并致力于保护您的隐私。

1. 数据收集

Moodist 不会收集、存储或处理您的任何个人数据。您的所有设置（例如音量和选定的声音）都使用 localStorage 本地存储在您的浏览器中。

2. 第三方服务

某些功能（如 Lofi 音乐）可能会嵌入来自 YouTube 等第三方的内容。这些第三方可能会根据其自身的隐私政策收集数据。Moodist 不控制或追踪这些数据。

3. 开源

本项目是开源的。您可以在 GitHub 上查看我们的代码以验证我们的隐私实践。
